Terms of use

Personal Mail Mirror is a personal, owner-operated utility, not a commercial email or cloud-backup service.

Permitted use

Only the person who owns or is authorized to access both the source mailbox and destination Gmail account should use the utility. The owner is responsible for reviewing and granting provider permissions.

No guaranteed availability or completeness

Mail copying depends on the workstation, Yahoo IMAP, Gmail API access, network reliability, available storage and message size limits. The mirror may pause or omit failed or oversized messages. It is not a guaranteed backup or an exact two-way synchronization service.

Account and data responsibility

The owner retains control over both accounts, local tokens, sync schedule and copied messages. Changing passwords, revoking authorization or disabling the workstation may interrupt the service.

Third-party services

Yahoo and Google operate their respective mail services under their own terms. Personal Mail Mirror is not endorsed by or affiliated with either company.
